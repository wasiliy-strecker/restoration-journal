## Purpose and stored data

Restoration Journal manages your personal entries. By default, the app stores names, material details, storage locations, colors, notes, internal entry identifiers and your language preference, along with an optional photo, project status and ordered checklist steps with completion states, locally in your computer’s user profile. Photos retain their original file contents and may include metadata. Entries can contain personal information if you enter it yourself. Processing serves only the functions you request to manage, search, export and import your collection.

## Local processing

The app requires no account or server. It does not transmit your collection or language preference to the developer or other recipients. It contains no advertising, telemetry, usage analytics or built-in cloud synchronization. Restoration Journal does not itself encrypt its stored collection or exported JSON files. Protection also depends on your user account, access permissions and any disk encryption you have enabled.

## Import, export and backups

The app reads selected JSON files and photos locally. A JSON export contains every saved entry, including an optional photo, project status and ordered checklist steps with completion states. It excludes internal entry identifiers and your language preference. Step identifiers, order and completion states are included. You choose the storage location and any sharing. Exported files are independent copies and are not updated or deleted by changes in the app. Synced folders, network drives or backup services may process or transfer these files outside the app. Their respective settings and privacy terms apply. Restoration Journal does not control these services.

## PDF output

A PDF contains the selected saved project or all saved projects with photos, colors, status and work steps. The app does not encrypt these PDF files. They are independent copies whose sharing and deletion you control.

## Retention and deletion

The app retains your collection until you delete entries or remove the stored app data. You can view, edit and delete entries in the app. To remove the entire local collection and language preference, close the app and remove its data folder. Storage locations are described in the included instructions. Export files and other backups must be deleted separately from their respective locations. Deleting an entry does not guarantee secure erasure of all previous file contents. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to remove all app data and external copies.

## Contact and support

If you voluntarily send us an email, we process your email address, message and attachments to handle your request. Your locally stored collection is not attached automatically. The legal basis for contract-related requests is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other requests it is our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We retain correspondence only as long as necessary to handle the request and meet statutory retention obligations.

## Your rights and other providers

Where applicable, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Use the privacy contact above to exercise these rights. The developer has no access to collections stored only on your computer. When you obtain or update the app through Apple App Store, Microsoft Store or GitHub, these providers may process data independently under their own privacy terms. This policy describes processing by Restoration Journal and will be updated when that processing changes.
